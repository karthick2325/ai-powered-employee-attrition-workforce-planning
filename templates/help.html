Ethical Workforce Intelligence & Human-in-the-Loop Principles

AttritionSense Technologies provides decision-support tools for authorized HR business partners. Non-Negotiable Rule: No employee should ever be automatically dismissed, disciplined, passed over for promotion, or denied career opportunities based on predictive risk probabilities or unsupervised cluster assignments.

  • Correlation is Not Causation: Risk factors such as overtime and tenure indicate statistical associations in historical data, not deterministic outcomes.
  • Protected Demographic Safeguards: Age and Gender are excluded from predictive features and clustering distance calculations as specified by the modeling policy. They may be used for authorized fairness audits.
  • Supportive Retention Interventions: Use risk insights to support workload reviews, career discussions, and training opportunities.

Supervised Attrition Prediction Methodology

6-Month Horizon

Target Definition: Attrition_Within_6M binary label (1 = exited within 6 months, 0 = stayed).

Class Imbalance: The documented baseline reports 5.79% positive exit prevalence (2,779 exits / 47,972 active records). Training-only class weighting and PR-AUC optimization are described in the methodology.

Holdout Evaluation: 80/20 stratified split (38,377 training / 9,595 test records). Test records should remain unresampled for unbiased holdout evaluation.

Baseline Benchmark: The documented Random Forest baseline reports Accuracy 76.9%, Recall 45.5%, ROC-AUC 0.680, and PR-AUC 0.121. Verify these figures against the latest evaluation results before relying on them.

5-Algorithm Unsupervised Segmentation

Clustering Benchmark

Feature Space: 11 numerical attributes and 3 categorical dimensions are documented, transformed using StandardScaler and OneHotEncoder. Verify the actual transformed feature count against the fitted pipeline.

Evaluated Algorithms: K-Means, Agglomerative Clustering (Ward), Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM), DBSCAN, and BIRCH.

Selection Rubric: Compare Silhouette score, Davies-Bouldin index, cluster size balance, and population coverage. Selection should be based on measured results.

Dynamic Archetypes: Derive cluster descriptions from actual cluster characteristics, such as salary, compa-ratio, and satisfaction, rather than hard-coded labels.
